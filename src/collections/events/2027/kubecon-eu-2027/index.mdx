Join Meshery at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2027 in Barcelona, Spain!

Join the Meshery project at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2027 from March 15th to March 18th, 2027 and get introduced to collaborative cloud native management and Meshery open source maintainers.

### Visit Us and Meet the Maintainers

Come say hello to the Meshery maintainers in Barcelona and learn about Meshery, the CNCF project for collaborative cloud native management. More details will be shared here closer to the event.

### Submit Your Meshery Talk to the Co-located Events

There's still time to share your Meshery story. The call for proposals for the CNCF-hosted co-located events on Monday, March 15, 2027 closes on Sunday, October 18, 2026 at 11:59 PM CEST.

### About Meshery

Meshery, the open source cloud native manager, enables collaborative configuration and management of your Kubernetes clusters, workloads, and CNCF projects. With Kanvas, Meshery provides an intuitive visual interface to design, deploy, and operate cloud native infrastructure with ease.
