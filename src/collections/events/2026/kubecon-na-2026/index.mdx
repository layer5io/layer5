KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026 takes place November 9-12, 2026 at the Salt Palace Convention Center in Salt Lake City, Utah.

The event brings together adopters and technologists from leading open source and cloud native communities, with 400+ sessions across Kubernetes, cloud native, and AI.

### Event Details

- **Dates:** November 9-12, 2026
  - **November 9:** Co-located events and pre-event programming
  - **November 10-12:** Main conference sessions
- **Location:** Salt Palace Convention Center, Salt Lake City, Utah
